🛠️ Prerequisites & Hardware Requirements

Before running the follow_along_scripts.txt, ensure your environment meets these specifications.
1. Hardware (Minimum Recommendation)

To run the full kube-prometheus-stack, your nodes need enough "headroom" for the Prometheus database and Grafana UI.

    3x Virtual Machines (or Physical Nodes):

        CPU: 2 Cores per node.

        RAM: 4GB per node (Prometheus is memory-intensive).

        Storage: 20GB+ SSD space.

    Host: A Proxmox VE (or similar) environment with a bridge network configured.

2. Network Configuration

    Subnet: A dedicated range for your lab (e.g., 10.3.200.0/24).

    Static IPs: Reserved IPs for your 3 nodes.

    MetalLB Range: A small block of unused IPs (e.g., .150 to .160) that are outside your DHCP pool but inside your subnet.

3. Software & Tools

    OS: Ubuntu 22.04/24.04 LTS or Fedora Server.

    Workstation: A laptop/PC with ssh and curl installed.

    Access: SSH key-pair generated on your workstation for passwordless access to the nodes.

💡 Why this setup?

    QEMU Guest Agent: Without this, Proxmox cannot gracefully shut down your VMs or report their IP addresses accurately in the GUI.

    No Swap: Kubernetes' scheduler expects to have total control over memory allocation. If the OS starts swapping to disk, performance becomes unpredictable, and K3s may fail to start.

    Cloud-Init: We use this to inject your SSH keys and network config automatically. It’s the first step toward the "Infrastructure as Code" goal we mention in the video.
